# Marginal Derivatives of a Quadratic Penalty

\\S = \lambda D'PD\\ is linear in \\\lambda\\ and free of the
coefficients, so \\\partial S/\partial\lambda = D'PD\\ and every higher
derivative is zero.

## Arguments

- pen:

  A `QuadraticPenalty` object.

- beta:

  A numeric vector of coefficients.

- theta:

  A named list of hyperparameters.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A named list of matrices, of vectors, or a logical.
